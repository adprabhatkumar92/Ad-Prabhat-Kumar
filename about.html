About Advocate Prabhat Kumar

Uncompromising Legal Representation & Strategic Client Advocacy

Professional Excellence & Practice Profile

Prabhat Kumar ek vishisht aur anubhavi Advocate hain, jo **Hon'ble High Court of Judicature at Allahabad** ke sath-sath **District & Civil Courts Mau aur Ballia** mein apni sateek aur prabhavi vikalp-heenta (litigation) ke liye jaane jaate hain[cite: 1]. Aapko apraadhik aur diwani mamlo mein gahan samajh praapt hai.

Apne kshetr ke top list advocates ki shreni mein ginne jaane wale Prabhat Kumar ji, qanooni daav-peech aur gahan research ke madhyam se apne clients ko nyay dilane mein agraṇi hain. Unki shaikshik yogyata mein M.Sc. aur LL.B. ki degree shamil hai[cite: 1], jo unhe jatil se jatil mamlo ko tarksangat aur aasan tarike se samajhne ki kshamta deti hai.
